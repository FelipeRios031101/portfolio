Digital Adoption & Route Prioritization

A weighted scoring model and executive dashboard suite built to prioritize commercial routes by their digital-adoption gap, with a client-universe drill-down and a funnel view of the onboarding journey per route.

About this tool

This dashboard prioritizes commercial routes based on how far their customer base is from full digital adoption. Each route gets a weighted priority score built from four adoption stages: not registered, registered but inactive, hybrid usage, and fully digital.

Selecting a route surfaces its full customer universe and a funnel chart showing exactly where the drop-off concentrates along the adoption journey — a design choice drawn from my background in CRM and inside sales, where spotting the bottleneck stage matters more than the aggregate number.

Data has been fully anonymized. Company name, region labels, and customer names are fictional; the underlying structure and proportions reflect a real commercial dataset.
